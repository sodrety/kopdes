# Record suspension fees separately from loan repayments

Accepted: a monthly regular-loan suspension defers an entirely unpaid installment and all subsequent installments by one calendar month. The original installment count and obligation remain intact. The effective repayment duration shown by the application is the contracted duration plus recorded suspensions. The final due date is extended by one month, including when the contracted duration is already at the normal product limit.

The member pays an additional monthly service fee. Use the approved monthly-admin snapshot, or the legacy total-admin snapshot divided by the contracted tenor and rounded to whole Rupiah. Never recalculate the fee from an extended tenor. The fee does not pay an installment or reduce the remaining loan obligation. Post a separate bank receipt, debit the configured repayment bank account and credit active posting revenue account 40201. Missing or inactive accounts reject the operation; no pending journal or inferred payment is permitted.

Tagihan provides an explicit action confirming the service fee has been received. XLSX exports add LoanSuspensions, one row per eligible regular loan, with stable loan/member IDs, statement month, calculated fee, action, reason and due dates. Blank actions are ignored. A selected action confirms fee receipt. The existing Tagihan paid/unpaid status continues to control savings and non-suspended loans. A new collection-after-suspension formula shows the adjusted payroll total without changing original exported totals. Returned amounts are never trusted as instructions to reprice loans.

App and Excel use the same transaction. Schedule updates, the suspension record and journal commit together; mixed-file validation failures roll back all operations. A unique loan/month key makes matching retries no-ops. Conflicting retries, a different member, tampered fee, partial/already paid installments, prior unpaid installments, and already-shifted historical seed events are rejected. Historical seed suspensions are not automatically charged: a zero historical event is not evidence that a service fee was received.

The suspension record preserves the actor, reason, original schedule and original/extended final dates. Independent journal reversal is blocked because it would leave an unpaid fee associated with an active schedule extension. A future coordinated cancellation must reverse the receipt and safely restore the affected schedule together. Ordinary unpaid rows remain arrears and do not create a suspension.
